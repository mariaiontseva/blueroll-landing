Source transparency

The proof behind the headline.

A living ledger of consequential claims used across Blueroll. Each entry gives the source, scope and limitation so a reader — or an answer engine — can verify it without taking our word for it.

UK requirements

Food safety management and allergens.

These entries describe official guidance. They are not claims that Blueroll alone creates compliance.

HACCP basis

UK food businesses need a written food safety management system based on HACCP principles.

GOV.UK says the system must be based on HACCP principles and that records are required as evidence of the system. A business may use a pack such as Safer Food, Better Business where appropriate.

GOV.UK source ↗
Business duty

Food businesses must make and follow a food safety management plan.

GOV.UK's hygiene guidance describes this as a core responsibility and notes enforcement outcomes can include a lower rating, closure or prosecution. Exact duties depend on the business and jurisdiction.

GOV.UK source ↗
14 allergens

UK food information rules specify 14 allergens that must be declared when used as ingredients.

The FSA list includes celery, cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, nuts, peanuts, sesame, soya and sulphur dioxide/sulphites above the stated threshold.

FSA source ↗
Scale and behaviour

Public-health and diner statistics.

These are population estimates and survey results. They do not measure the performance of Blueroll customers.

2.4 million

Estimated UK foodborne illness cases per year.

The FSA announced research estimating around 2.4 million UK cases annually. This is a modelled national estimate, not a count of laboratory-confirmed cases and not a restaurant-only figure.

FSA source ↗
£10.4 billion

Approximate annual UK burden of foodborne illness.

Our Food 2023 reports an approximate £10.4bn annual burden. This is an economic estimate that includes human and financial costs; it is not annual restaurant revenue lost.

FSA report (PDF) ↗
41%

Respondents who consider the food hygiene rating when deciding where to eat out.

Reported in the FSA's Food and You 2, Wave 8 key findings. It is a survey response for the specified wave and should not be presented as a causal revenue effect.

FSA survey ↗
