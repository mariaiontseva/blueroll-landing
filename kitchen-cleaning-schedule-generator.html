Using your schedule

What to put in a kitchen cleaning schedule

Walk through the kitchen

List the surfaces, equipment, touch points and storage areas you actually use. Include the items that are easy to miss: handles, seals, controls and the space behind equipment. Remove starter jobs that do not apply and add the ones your kitchen needs.

Write the method, not just the job

Open each job's extra details. Record who does it, how it is cleaned, which product is used and its required contact time. Take dilution, contact time, rinsing and equipment precautions from the relevant manufacturer instructions.

Set a useful frequency

After-use and each-shift tasks belong alongside daily, weekly and less frequent jobs. Use the “When” field for a particular day or trigger. The weekly sheet gives your team a place to sign off work; it does not decide how often a task is needed.

Review the completed sheet

Explain the schedule to staff, check that the work is done properly and record missed jobs and corrective action in your usual diary. Update the schedule when equipment, cleaning products or working practices change.

Guidance: GOV.UK: cleaning in food businesses and the FSA SFBB safe method: your cleaning schedule. The FSA method covers listing items, cleaning methods, frequencies and staff supervision.

Cleaning schedule generator questions

Can I save my cleaning schedule as a PDF?

Yes. Select Print or save PDF, then choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog. You can also export CSV to edit the schedule in a spreadsheet.

Do I need an email address or a Blueroll account?

No. The generator, printing and CSV export are free without an email address or an account. Your entries stay in this browser tab; print or export them before closing it.

Is a cleaning schedule the same as a HACCP plan?

A cleaning schedule describes cleaning tasks, methods and responsibilities. It is one supporting record within your food safety management system. Your HACCP-based plan also needs to address the hazards and controls relevant to your food operations.
