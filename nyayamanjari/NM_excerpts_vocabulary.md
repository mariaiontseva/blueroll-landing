Covers all three excerpts of `NM_pdf_excerpts_reader.html` (NM Vol II pp. 4–5; NM Vol II pp. 139–142; Mīmāṃsānyāyaprakāśa intro). Forms are given as they occur (after sandhi resolution); compounds are lemmatised under their head-nouns with notable members noted in glosses.

Totals: 37 finite verbs · 36 participles/verbal adjs · 48 indeclinables (incl. absolutives & infinitives) · 133 nouns + 18 adjectives + 16 pronominal stems.
