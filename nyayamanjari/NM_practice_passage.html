Подобран как близнец двух твоих отрывков, но **без стихов** и в более доступной форме. Опровергается *ākṛti-вāда* — мīмāṃсака-теория, что значение слова «корова» — это её внешняя конфигурация. Та же лексика (śabda, padārtha, vyakti, jāti, pratyakṣa), знаменитый «корова-пример», классический ход *‘yad uktam — tad ayuktam’*, далее три ablative-довода.

Translation

Even in everyday speech people refer to ākṛti as having the nature of the arrangement of parts (avayava-sanniveśātmikā), citing: ‘where there is ākṛti, there qualities reside.’ Hence ākṛti is said to be precisely the arrangement of parts.

Определение через мирской узус (loko ’pi). *Sanniveśa-ātmikā* — bahuvrīhi-f. «состоящая в расположении». Сандхи: sanniveśa + ātmikā → -śātmikā (a+ā→ā).

And whether or not śabdārthatā applies to it deserves examination — we do not, like the Jaiminīyas, dismiss it as self-evidently fitting. Since the word gauḥ is used in the joint presence of vyakti, ākṛti and jāti, the question is: what is its referent?

Классификационный поворот: три кандидата — vyakti, ākṛti, jāti. *Śabdārthatā* — абстрактное от śabdārtha. *Tasyāḥ* (gen. f.) отсылает к ākṛti. *Sannidhau* loc. «при одновременном присутствии».

The ākṛti-vādins state first: the determination of a word’s meaning rests on prayoga and pratipatti. Whatever object the competent (buddhāḥ) use the word gauḥ for in their dealings, and whatever object hearers grasp from it — that is its meaning.

*Pūrvapakṣa*. Двойной критерий *prayoga-pratipattibhyām* (instr. dual) — стандартный приём и в Mīmāṃsā, и в Nyāya. *Buddhāḥ* «сведущие [носители]».

Now: gauḥ is not used for a maned creature (kesarādimat — i.e. the lion), but is used for a dewlap-bearing one (sāsnādimat). Hence it is understood as having for its object precisely that unique configuration (asādhāraṇa-sanniveśa).

«Корова-пример». *Kesarādimati / sāsnādimati* — loc. sg. от bahuvrīhi «обладающий гривой / подгрудком». *Asādhāraṇa* «уникальный, не общий с посторонним».

Words such as gauḥ are used with respect to a perceptual object (pratyakṣa-viṣaye), and perception itself has ākṛti as its object. For the configuration of a cow’s body (gopiṇḍa-sanniveśa) is grasped by the sense-organ itself as distinct from that of a horse’s body. It is by that very configuration that things are mutually distinguished.

Мост к Āhnika 2: ākṛti-вāдин апеллирует к pratyakṣa. *Indriyeṇa* (instr.) подчёркнуто. *Itaretara-vailakṣaṇyam* «взаимное различение».

Therefore, they say, a word operating in the field of perception must operate upon ākṛti alone; and its connection with actions such as preṣaṇa (the sending off of an animal) will obtain for ākṛti by way of the individual (vyakti-dvāraka). — *So they have said; but this is wrong* (tad ayuktam).

Конец *pūrvapakṣa* + стандартный мост *yad uktam — tad ayuktam*. *Vyakti-dvāraka* «через посредство индивида» — попытка спасти ākṛti при практической связи с конкретными животными.

[Three refutations:] (i) because a different configuration is seen in each individual (prati-vyakti bhinna-saṃsthāna-darśanāt); (ii) because the establishing of a word–form connection is impossible — owing to the infinitude (ānantya) and deviation (vyabhicāra) [of cases]; (iii) gauḥ does not denote any fixed Śābaleya configuration, since the Bāhuleya configuration is also observed [to be so called] even in its absence.

Три ablative-основания (*-darśanāt, -asaṃbhavāt, -darśanāt*) — типичная naiyāyika-арифметика. *Śābaleya* — пятнистая, *Bāhuleya* — рыжая корова.
