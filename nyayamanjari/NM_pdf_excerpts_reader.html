Āhnika 5 (śabda-parīkṣā), opening · padaṃ-vākyam, sub-divisions of pada

Vol II · p. 4

[śabde dvividhaḥ][शब्दे द्विविधः]

dvividhaḥ śabdaḥ padaṃ vākyaṃ ca | tatra padārthapūrvaktvāt vākyārthasya prathamaṃ padārtho nirdiśya nirūpyate ‖

[padam api dvividham][पदमपि द्विविधम्]

padaṃ ca dvividhaṃ nāma ākhyātaṃ ca | upasarganipātakarmapravacanīyānām api nāmasvevāv antarbhāvam ācakṣate | tad uktaṃ [suptiṅantaṃ padam] iti | ihāpi sūtrakṛd āha [te vibhaktyantāḥ padaṃ] iti | tatra tiṅantapadārthacintā vākyārthavicārāvasara evakariṣyate tadaupayikatvāt | subantānāṃ tv artho 'yam ucyate ‖

Vol II · p. 5

[nāmapadāni caturvidhāni][नामपदानि चतुर्विधानि]

te ca caturvidhāḥ subantāḥ padātmanaḥ śabdā bhavanti jātiśabdāḥ kriyāśabdāḥ guṇaśabdāḥ dravyaśabdāḥ iti ‖ tatra gavādijātiśabdānāṃ gotvādijātyavacchinnaṃ vyaktimātram arthaḥ yas tadvān iti naiyyāyikagṛhe gīyate ‖

[gatvādiṣu jātivācakatvākṣepaḥ][गत्वादिषु जातिवाचकत्वाक्षेपः]

nanu śuklādiguṇādhikaraṇaṃ kriyāśrayaś ca dravyaṃ vyaktiḥ sāsnādyavayavasanniveśātmikā ākṛtiḥ śābaleyādisakalagopiṇḍasādhāraṇaṃ rūpaṃ jātir iti vyaktyākṛtijātisannidhāne samuccārita eṣa gośabdaḥ katham itaratiraskāreṇa tadvanmātravacanatām avalambeta

[jātivicāropakṣepaḥ][जातिविचारोपक्षेपः]

āha vitatā khalv iyaṃ kathā carcayiṣyate tāvat | idaṃ tu cintyatām jāter eva pramāṇātītatvana śaśaśṛṅgavad avidyamānatvāt kathaṃ tadvān padārtho bhaviṣyati

Excerpt 2 — Vol II, pp. 139–142

Āhnika 5 (śabda-parīkṣā), closing · vākyārtha and the end of the āhnika

Vol II · p. 139

[saṃsargasya aśābdatvaśaṅketyavaśeṣaḥ][संसर्गस्य अशाब्दत्वशङ्केत्यवशेषः]

nanu saṃsargo 'pi na śabdārthaḥ | satyam | sa hi śabdasyādhidheyo na bhavati na tu tato na pratīyate | abhidheyaḥ kathaṃ pratīyate iti cet etad agre nirṇīṣyate | vyavacchede tu na sā gatiḥ | tasmāt saṃsṛṣṭāḥ padārthāḥ vākyārtha iti sthitam ‖

[phalakarūpamānānupasthiter vākyārthatvam][फलकरूपमानानुपस्थितेर्वाक्यार्थत्वम्]

atha vā guṇībhūtetarapadārthānugṛhītaḥ eka eva pradhānabhūtaḥ padārtho vākyārtha ity ekākārapratītibalād upeyatām | ekas tv ayam asāv artha iti na nirṇetuṃ śakyate | yadi tv avaśyam ekasya kasyacid adhiṣekakalaśo dātavyaḥ tat phalasyaiva dīyatām | na hi niṣprayojanaṃ kiṃcid vākyam uccaryate ‖

kvacit sākṣātpadopāttaṃ kvacit prakaraṇāgatam |
kvacid ālocanālabhyaṃ phalaṃ sarvatra gamyate ‖

sakalena ca kārakakalāpena kriyā nirvartyate | kriyayā ca phalam | na tu phalenānyat kim api nirvartyata iti pradhānatvāt phalam eva vākyārthaḥ ‖

[phalasyaiva prādhānyapakṣaḥ][फलस्यैव प्राधान्यपक्षः]

nanu phalam api puruṣārtham iti puruṣaḥ pradhānaṃ syāt | naitad evam | phalaṃ sukhātmakatvāt puruṣāśritaṃ bhavati sukhādīnām ātmaguṇatvāt | na caitāvatā puruṣaḥ pradhānam | so 'pi hi phalārtham eva yatate | bhāvanā tāvat

Vol II · p. 140

phalaniṣṭha eva vyāpāraḥ niyogasyāpi phalaṃ vinā na pravartakatvam ity uktam | kriyāyā api kevalāyā vākyārthatvam apāstam ‖

tasmāt phalasya sādhyatvāt sarvatra tadavarjanāt |
kriyādīnāṃ ca tādarthyāt tasya vākyārthateṣyate ‖

[kriyāyā api phaladoṣaśaṅkā][क्रियाया अपि फलदोषशङ्का]

nanu phalasya svargādeḥ nisargataḥ siddharūpatvāt kārakais saha saṃbandho na prāpnoti | siddhasya ca kaḥ saṃbandhaḥ | kriyāgarbha iti cet tarhi phalam api kārakāṇy api kriyayā saṃbadhyate ko viśeṣaḥ | satyam | paraṃ tu kārakāṇi sādhanatvena phalaṃ tu sādhyatvena | kriyayā hi phalaṃ sādhyate na phalena kriyety ataḥ phalasyaiva prādhānyam iti siddham ‖

anyonyasaṃgativiśeṣita eva yasmāt
vākyārthabhāvam upayāti padārthapuṃjaḥ |
etac ca cetasi nidhāya tato na bhinnaṃ
vākyārtham abhyakadhita ca kaścana sūtrakāraḥ ‖
prādhānyayogād atha vā phalasya
vākyārthatā tatra satāṃ hi yatnaḥ |
prayojanaṃ sūtrakṛtā tad eva
pravartakatvena kilopadiṣṭam ‖

[udyogasya vākyārthatvākṣepaḥ][उद्योगस्य वाक्यार्थत्वाक्षेपः]

apare punaḥ liṅādiśabdaśravaṇe sati samupajāyamānām ātmaspandaviśeṣaṃ udyogaṃ nāma vākyārtham ācakṣate | tatsvarūpaṃ tu vayaṃ na jānīmaḥ ko 'yam ātma-

Vol II · p. 141

spando nāmeti | buddhisukhaduḥkhecchādveṣaprayatnadharmādharmasaṃskārā hi nava ātmano guṇā viśeṣaguṇā bhavanti nānye | tatrāyam ātmaspandaḥ buddhir vā syāt prayatno vā icchādveṣayor anyataro vā | anye tu vikalpayitum api na yuktāḥ | tatra yadi buddhir ātmaspanda ucyate tarhi pratibhā vākyārtha ity uktaṃ bhavati na nūtanaṃ kiṃcid utprekṣitam etat ‖ atha prayatna ātmaspandaḥ tarhi bhāvanāyā nāmāntarakaraṇam udyoga iti ‖ atha icchādveṣayor anyataro 'sau tarhi sukhecchā duḥkhajihāsā vā vākyārtha iti akṣapādapakṣa evāyaṃ nāpūrvaṃ kiṃcid ‖ athāpi bhāṭṭaparikalpito vyāpāra ātmaspandaḥ so 'pi bhāvanaiva nārthāntaram ‖ arthānuṣṭheyaḥ prerakaḥ kaścid artha udyogaḥ sa tarhi niyoga eva upasargānyatvam idaṃ na vastv anyat ‖

tasmād aśrutapūrveṇa kṛtam udyogaparvaṇā |
sa bhāratamanuṣyāṇāṃ gocaro na tu mādṛśām ‖

[vākyapadīyokta-pratibhāyāḥ vākyārthatvapakṣaḥ][वाक्यपदीयोक्तः प्रतिभायाः वाक्यार्थत्वपक्षः]

anyais tu pratibhā vākyārtha iṣyate | tatpakṣas tu saṃsarganirbhāsajñānanirākaraṇena prāg eva pratikṣiptaḥ ‖

pratibhā khalu vijñānaṃ tac ca śabdena jāyate |
na tu śabdasya viṣayaḥ rūpadhīr iva cakṣuṣaḥ ‖

bāhyasya viṣayasyābhāvāt saiva viṣaya iti cet na tasya samarthitatvāt ‖

Vol II · p. 142

[muktasaṅgatyarthasya śabdaprāmāṇyopayogitvam][मुक्तसङ्गत्यर्थस्य शब्दप्रामाण्योपयोगित्वम्]

yo 'pi [vyāghra āyātaḥ] ity ukte śūrakātaranarādhikaraṇanānākārakāryotpādaḥ sa bāhye 'rthe vyāghrāgamānādau pratipanne vāsanānusāreṇa bhavan na pratibhāmātrahetuko bhavati | tasya hi jñāyamāno 'rthaḥ karaṇam na tadjñānamātram | arthas tadānīṃ nāstīti cet vipralambhavākyam idaṃ asatyārthaṃ bhaviṣyati na tv abāhyaviṣayaṃ tat | yathā 'vasthite vanitātmani bāhye 'rthe vāsanānusāreṇa kuṇapa iti kāminīti bhakṣyam iti pratibhā bhavanti tathā śabdārtho 'pi vyāghrāgamane 'vagate śūrāṇām utsāhaḥ kātarāṇabhayam ityādi kāryaṃ bhavati | na tv etāvatā pratibhā śabdārtho bhavitum arhati | tasmāt vākyaprayojanatvena vā yadi pratibhā vākyārthaḥ kathyate kathyatāṃ nāma na tv asau śabdasyābhidheyā | anabhidheyā 'pi saṃsargavat vākyārtha iti cet tatrāpy ayuktam | saṃsṛṣṭā vākyārthaḥ na saṃsargaḥ | evam ihāpi pratibhāvanto 'rthā vākyārthaḥ na pratibheti ‖ śabdasya ca pratyakṣavat vartamānārthaniṣṭatvābhāvāt anāgatādyarthābhidhāyino 'rthasannidhānena pratibhāparatvaṃ yad ucyate tad apy ayuktam anāgatādiviṣayatve 'pi tasyārthaviṣayatvaṃ prasādhitam iti kṛtaṃ vistareṇa ‖

vākyārthaḥ paramārtha eva tad ayaṃ no kalpanānirmitaḥ
tadvān apy uditaḥ padasya viṣayas tenārthasaṃsparśinā |
aprāmāṇyam ataś ca bāhyaviṣayābhāvena yad varṇyate
tat śabdasya nirastam ity akaluṣaṃ prāmāṇyam asya sthitam ‖
iti śrījayantabhaṭṭakṛtau nyāyamañjaryāṃ pañcamam āhnikam ‖
इति श्रीजयन्तभट्टकृतौ न्यायमञ्जर्यां पञ्चममाह्निकम् ॥

Excerpt 3 — Mīmāṃsānyāyaprakāśa, Introduction

Āpadeva's Mīmāṃsānyāyaprakāśa (Āpadevī) — a separate work, given here as a parallel on śābdī bhāvanā / apauruṣeyatva of the Veda

ākhyātatvaṃ ca daśasu lakāreṣu vidyate, liṅtvaṃ punaḥ kevalaṃ liṅy eva | tatrākhyātatvaliṅtvābhyāṃ bhāvanaivocyate | sā ca dvividhā, śābdī bhāvanā ārthī bhāvanā ceti | tatra puruṣapravṛttyanukūlo bhāvakavyāpāraviśeṣaḥ śābdī bhāvanā | sā ca liṅtvāṃśenocyate, liṅśravaṇe 'ayaṃ māṃ pravartayati, matpravṛttyanukūlavyāpāravān ayam' iti niyamena pratīyamānatvāt | yac ca yasmāt pratīyate tat tasya vācyam, yathā gośabdasya gotvam | sa ca pravṛttyanukūlavyāpāraviśeṣo loke puruṣaniṣṭho 'bhiprāyaviśeṣaḥ | vede tu puruṣābhāvāl liṅādiśabdaniṣṭha eva | na hi vedaḥ puruṣanirmitaḥ |

vedasyādhyayanaṃ sarvaṃ gurvadhyayanapūrvakam |
vedādhyayanasāmānyād adhunādhyayanaṃ yathā ‖
