Handling an EHO visit with Blueroll

How to show the Environmental Health Officer everything they need — calmly, from one tablet. Everything you record in Blueroll each day is your inspection evidence; you don't prepare for the visit, you just keep logging and it's all here.

Officers score you on three things. Blueroll gives you evidence for all of them — and it's strongest on “Confidence in Management” (your records & food-safety system), which is where most sites gain or lose their rating.

1 What the officer scores

Your Food Hygiene Rating is built from these three areas. Know which screen backs each one.

Food Hygiene

How food is handled — temperatures, cooking, chilling, cross-contamination, cleaning.

Reports · temperature logs

Structure & Cleanliness

The premises — cleaning, pest control, layout, facilities, maintenance.

Cleaning records · pest docs

Confidence in Management ★

Your records & system — proof you know and control your risks. Blueroll’s home ground.

HACCP Pack (SFBB)

2 When the officer arrives

Calm, organised and open scores better than nervous. You drive the tablet — don’t just hand it over.

  1. Greet them, bring the manager to the tablet and log in as Manager.
  2. Set the tone: “We manage all our food safety on Blueroll — I can show you whatever you need on here.”
  3. You walk them through each screen — don’t open the profile and leave them to hunt (that reads as disorganised).
  4. If they want a copy, use Export → PDF (HACCP Pack and Reports both export a clean document).

3 Your food-safety system — the HACCP Pack (SFBB)

This is the centrepiece. Blueroll’s HACCP Pack is a live Safer Food, Better Business document, auto-built from the data you already record (recipes, checklists, documents, suppliers, team). It’s laid out in the five SFBB sections the officer expects — open it and walk through them.

1 · Cross-Contamination
  • Personal Hygiene
  • Cloths
  • Separating Foods (raw vs ready-to-eat)
  • Food Allergies (allergen matrix)
  • Contamination Prevention
  • Pest Control
Auto-filled from: recipes (raw ingredients), suppliers (delivery days), allergen matrix, pest-control document.
2 · Cleaning
  • Handwashing
  • Cleaning Effectively
  • Clean As You Go
  • Cleaning Schedule (incl. beer lines & kegs)
Auto-filled from: your cleaning checklists + uploaded cleaning schedule.
3 · Chilling
  • Chilled Storage (fridges < 5 °C, freezers < −18 °C)
  • Chilling Down Hot Food
  • Defrosting
  • Freezing
Auto-filled from: temperature checklists + recipe chilling / defrosting / freezing methods.
4 · Cooking
  • Cooking Safely (core ≥ 75 °C)
  • Extra Care Foods (eggs, rice, shellfish…)
  • Reheating (≥ 85 °C)
  • Menu Checks
  • Hot Holding (> 63 °C)
  • Ready-to-Eat
Auto-filled from: recipe cooking / reheating / hot-holding methods and temperatures.
5 · Management
  • Opening & Closing Checks
  • Suppliers
  • Stock Control
  • Training (certificates)
  • Temperature Probes (calibration)
  • Daily Diary
Auto-filled from: checklist templates, suppliers table, team + training documents, calibration checklist.

5 The 4-Weekly HACCP Review

Officers don’t just want records — they want proof the system is kept current. SFBB requires a review every 4 weeks, and Blueroll builds it in.

What it is

A short manager sign-off every 4 weeks confirming each SFBB section is still accurate. It runs as the default “4-Weekly HACCP Review” checklist — one line per section (Cross-Contamination up to date, Cleaning up to date, and so on).

Where to show it

The HACCP Pack shows a “Last reviewed: {date}” banner at the top. Blueroll reminds the manager 3 days before, on the day, and if it’s overdue — so it’s never missed. Open the banner to prove it’s current.

If the officer asks “how do you keep your food-safety system up to date?” — point straight at the Last reviewed date in the HACCP Pack and the 4-weekly review checklist in your history. That single answer is worth a lot under “Confidence in Management”.

6 What to open for each question

The officer asks → you open this.

“Temperature records” (fridges / freezers)
Reports filter the Fridge & Freezer templates + a date range → history & compliance %. Or Checklists → History.
“Cooking / reheating / hot-holding temps”
Reports (Temperature / Cooling templates) and HACCP Pack → Cooking.
“Cleaning schedule & records”
Reports (Deep Clean / Cleaning Schedule) + HACCP Pack → Cleaning.
“Your HACCP / SFBB system”
HACCP Pack — the whole document, auto-built. Export → PDF for a copy.
“How do you keep it up to date?”
HACCP Pack “Last reviewed” banner (4-weekly review) + Diary.
“Allergen information”
Allergen Matrix (per dish) + Menu.
“Pest control”
Documents (contract / reports) + HACCP Pack → Pest Control.
“Staff training & supervision”
Documents (certificates), Team (roles), HACCP Pack → Training.
“Deliveries & suppliers (cold chain)”
Deliveries (temps on receipt) + Suppliers.
“What do you do when something’s wrong?”
Incidents + flagged checklist items + the Corrective Action Log. A logged problem with a fix beats a blank record.

7 Keep-ready — before any visit

If these are true every day, you’re inspection-ready at any moment:

  • Today’s opening & closing checks completed and signed off
  • Fridge/freezer, cooking, cooling and hot-water temperatures logged
  • Anything flagged has a corrective action recorded against it
  • The 4-weekly HACCP review is not overdue (check the HACCP Pack banner)
  • Training certificates & pest-control documents are current in Documents
  • The Allergen Matrix matches the current menu
  • Probes calibrated this week

8 Do & don’t

Do

  • Drive the tablet yourself and walk them through it
  • Show completed history, not just today
  • Quote the standard, then show it being logged
  • Use Export → PDF if they want copies
  • Be honest about a gap — and show the corrective action you took

Don’t

  • Hand over the login and walk away
  • Back-fill or edit records during the visit
  • Hide a flagged item — a recorded problem + fix scores better than a blank
  • Guess an answer — open the screen and show it
