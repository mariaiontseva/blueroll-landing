Why display your food hygiene rating?

Your food hygiene rating is one of the first things customers check before visiting your restaurant. Displaying it prominently on your website builds instant trust and can increase bookings by up to 30%.

Builds customer trust

A visible 4 or 5-star food hygiene rating tells potential customers that your kitchen meets high standards. It's social proof that works 24/7 on your website.

Required by law in some areas

In Wales and Northern Ireland, displaying your food hygiene rating is a legal requirement. In England, it's voluntary — but businesses that display it signal confidence and transparency.

Our widget pulls data directly from the Food Standards Agency register. When your rating changes after an inspection, the widget updates automatically — no maintenance needed.

How the food hygiene rating scheme works

The Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (FHRS) is run by the Food Standards Agency in partnership with local authorities. Environmental Health Officers inspect food businesses and rate them from 0 to 5 based on three criteria:

  • Hygienic food handling — how food is prepared, cooked, cooled, reheated, and stored
  • Physical condition of the business — cleanliness, layout, lighting, ventilation, and pest control
  • Food safety management — HACCP procedures, staff training records, and temperature monitoring systems

A rating of 5 means hygiene standards are "very good." A rating of 0 means "urgent improvement is necessary."
