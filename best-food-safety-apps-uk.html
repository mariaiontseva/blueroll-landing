Best food safety apps for UK kitchens (2026)

If you run one small kitchen and only need the Safer Food, Better Business (SFBB) diary on a phone, The UK Food Safety App is free and SFBB+ costs £4.99 a month per premises after a 90-day trial. Hubl, Blueroll, SFBB Pro, Leafe and Trail start at £19 to £38 a site a month and add team logins, allergens and multi-site views, some on higher plans only. Food Safety Guru, FoodDocs and Navitas Safety suit businesses that want HACCP written for them, traceability or sensors.

Blueroll makes this page and is one of the eleven apps. They are listed by the kind of kitchen each suits, not by preference, with prices from each vendor's own page or app store listing, checked on 19 November 2026.

One small kitchen, little or no budget

Three ways to keep the SFBB diary on a phone for nothing or for a few pounds a month.

The UK Food Safety App

FreeNo paid tier and no ads.

Nothing to pay, so no trial.

Best for
One small kitchen that wants the whole SFBB pack on a phone at no cost.
What it does
The SFBB daily diary and four-weekly review, fridge, cooking, cooling and hot-holding records, cleaning, the allergen chart, training and suppliers, printed onto the FSA's own forms as one PDF. It also shows any business's FSA hygiene rating.
Limitation
No view across several sites is listed, and the App Store names an individual developer as the provider, so check support and exports before you rely on it. It is a different product from ukfoodsafety.app, which is SafeKitchen UK.
Works on
iOS, Android and the web.
Sources
App Store listing, fsyay.co.uk

SafeKitchen UK

Free planFor one site and one user, or £9.99, £19.99 or £29.99 a site a month on paid plans.

90 days free on paid plans, no card.

Best for
A kitchen that wants three months of full digital records before paying anything.
What it does
The SFBB daily diary and monthly review, temperature, cooling and delivery checks, cleaning, a 14-allergen matrix, training records, incidents and a 90-day report for the EHO. Professional covers unlimited sites and users.
Limitation
The free plan keeps 30 days of records and Starter has one user per site. The site at ukfoodsafety.app calls itself both UK Food Safety and SafeKitchen UK; it is not Safe Kitchen at safekitchenapp.co.uk, a separate app from £14.99 a month.
Works on
In the browser on phones, tablets and computers.

SFBB+

£4.99A month per premises, VAT included.

90 days free with no payment details (3 calendar months through the App Store).

Best for
The SFBB pack on a phone at the lowest paid price.

A digital version of the FSA's SFBB pack for caterers, adapted for Scotland's CookSafe and Ireland's Safe Catering. It keeps the daily diary and temperature records, covers several premises and staff, reminds you when a diary is missed and can email the whole pack to the inspecting officer. Made by All Environmental Health Services Ltd.

No allergen matrix or recipe tools are listed, and larger businesses with several premises are asked to contact the company about licensing.

Questions owners ask

Is there a free SFBB app?

Yes. The UK Food Safety App is free on iOS, Android and the web, with no paid tier, and keeps the whole SFBB pack. SafeKitchen UK has a free plan for one site and one user that keeps 30 days of records. SFBB+ is free for 90 days, then £4.99 a month per premises. The SFBB pack itself is free to download from GOV.UK.

Do EHOs accept digital records?

The Food Standards Agency's SFBB guidance on GOV.UK says completed diary pages can be stored electronically or as printed copies until your next visit from a council food safety officer, who may ask to see them alongside your pack. The guidance does not name or approve any app, so make sure the one you choose lets you show or export your records on the day.

Which app is cheapest per site?

The UK Food Safety App costs nothing. Of the paid plans, SFBB+ is lowest at £4.99 a month per premises including VAT, then SafeKitchen UK Starter at £9.99 a site with one user, and Hubl Tall at £19 a site plus VAT. Blueroll is £24.99 a site with unlimited team members. Food Safety Guru and Navitas Safety do not publish prices. These are the prices published on 19 November 2026.

Which app suits a restaurant group with several sites?

Trail, FoodDocs and Navitas Safety are built around head-office oversight of many sites. Blueroll, Hubl, SFBB Pro, Food Safety Guru and SafeKitchen UK also show every site in one place. For a group, multiply the per-site price by your number of sites and check the minimum term: Trail's plans run for 12 months.

Do I need an app at all?

No. The SFBB pack is free from GOV.UK and you can print the diary and fill it in by hand. An app helps when several people share the checks, when you want reminders, or when you run more than one site.
