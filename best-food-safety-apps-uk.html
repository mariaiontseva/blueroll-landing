Food safety app, HACCP app or food hygiene app?

Usually the same thing: an app that keeps the records your food safety system asks for, such as fridge temperatures, cooking, cleaning and the SFBB diary. A HACCP app may also help you write the plan itself, the hazards and critical control points. FoodDocs, Food Safety Guru and Paddl do that, and the FSA's free MyHACCP tool builds a plan without the daily records.

A food hygiene rating app is different. It looks up a business's rating on the FSA register and keeps no records. Our rating checker does that for free.

How to choose

  • Who fills it in. If several people share the checks you need team logins. If staff won't install an app, look at apps that run in the browser.
  • Which plan has allergens. On Hubl, Leafe and FoodDocs allergens start on a higher plan, so compare the "Allergens from" column, not the starting price.
  • What a year costs. Multiply by your sites and check the term: Trail runs for 12 months with 90 days' notice.
  • What the inspector sees. Make sure you can show or export your records on the day of the visit.
  • Who sets it up. Lemon and Blueroll set you up from photos of your current sheets. Elsewhere setup is usually yours to do.

Questions owners ask

What is the best food safety app in the UK?

It depends on who fills it in and how many sites you run. For one small kitchen, The UK Food Safety App is free and SFBB+ costs £4.99 a month. For a team, Lemon and Hubl start at £19 a site, Blueroll is £24.99 with allergens and free setup, and Culinary Key is £28 to £40. Groups usually look at Trail, FoodDocs or Navitas Safety. We make Blueroll, so compare the table rather than take our word for it.

Is there a free food safety app?

Yes. The UK Food Safety App is free on iOS, Android and the web, with no paid tier, and keeps the whole SFBB pack. SafeKitchen UK has a free plan for one site and one user that keeps 30 days of records, and FoodDocs builds a HACCP plan for free. The SFBB pack itself is free to download from GOV.UK.

Is there a HACCP app?

Yes. Most apps here keep the daily HACCP records: temperatures, cleaning, deliveries and checks. FoodDocs, Food Safety Guru and Paddl also help you write the plan itself, and the FSA's free MyHACCP tool builds one for small food manufacturers.

Do EHOs accept digital records?

The Food Standards Agency's SFBB guidance on GOV.UK says completed diary pages can be stored electronically or as printed copies until your next visit from a council food safety officer, who may ask to see them alongside your pack. The guidance does not name or approve any app, so make sure the one you choose lets you show or export your records on the day.

Which app is cheapest per site?

The UK Food Safety App costs nothing. Of the paid plans, SFBB+ is lowest at £4.99 a month per premises including VAT, then Food-Safety.app at £6.99 and SafeKitchen UK Starter at £9.99 with one user. For a team, Lemon and Hubl Tall are £19 a site and Blueroll is £24.99. SFBB Pro drops to £12.95 for each site after the first. Food Safety Guru and Navitas Safety do not publish prices. These are the prices published on 25 November 2026.

Which app suits a restaurant group with several sites?

Trail, FoodDocs and Navitas Safety are built around head-office oversight of many sites. Blueroll, Hubl, SFBB Pro, Culinary Key, Lemon and SafeKitchen UK also show every site in one place. Multiply the per-site price by your number of sites and check the minimum term: Trail's plans run for 12 months. More on running food safety across several sites.

Can staff do checks without installing an app?

Yes. SafeKitchen UK, Trail, Culinary Key and the NCASS system run in a phone's browser, so staff open a link instead of installing an app.

Is a food hygiene rating app the same as a food safety app?

No. A rating app looks up a business's score on the FSA register and keeps no records. A food safety app keeps your own records for the inspector. You can look up any rating free with our rating checker, and see how ratings are worked out.

Do I need an app at all?

No. The SFBB pack is free from GOV.UK and you can print the diary and fill it in by hand. An app helps when several people share the checks, when you want reminders, or when you run more than one site.
