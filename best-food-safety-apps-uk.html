UK buyer's guide · 2026

The best food safety app is the one that fits your operation.

Seven credible options, compared using public facts rather than a made-up score. Start with the job you need done: a simple digital SFBB record, a food-safety-first system, broader restaurant operations, or an enterprise inspection platform.

Short answer

Pick by use case, not by feature count.

Our view based on the publicly documented offer. A live trial with the people who actually complete checks is more useful than any comparison table.

Food safety first

Blueroll or FoodDocs

Blueroll is the lean UK-priced option for HACCP records, recipes, allergens and site reporting. FoodDocs is a more expensive specialist with AI-led food safety plan tooling.

Simple SFBB

SFBB+

The lowest published subscription here and closely framed around the FSA's SFBB model. Best when the core need is a straightforward digital diary rather than a broader operations suite.

Broader operations

Trail or Leafe

Consider these when food safety is one part of a wider daily-operations requirement such as tasks, rotas, inventory or deeper group workflows.

Cross-industry

SafetyCulture

A broad inspection platform suited to organisations that need configurable audits across food safety, facilities, health and safety, and other functions.

Expert support

Food Safety Guru

Worth evaluating when direct specialist food-safety support matters more than a self-serve public price.

Transparent verdicts

Where each product may fit best.

Blueroll

Consider it if: you run an independent UK restaurant or a growing group and want food safety, allergens and records in one focused product with unlimited team members. Look elsewhere if: you need enterprise SSO, a large integration catalogue or an all-industry audit platform.

SFBB+

Consider it if: price and a simple digital SFBB diary are the priority. Look more widely if: recipes, automated allergen workflows, documents or multi-site management are central to the buying decision.

Trail

Consider it if: a hospitality operations platform and mature group workflows justify the higher published site price. Compare carefully if: the main need is a lean food-safety system for a small number of sites.

Leafe

Consider it if: you want restaurant operations, compliance and workforce functions under tiered plans. Check the maths: team limits and VAT materially affect the entry price.

FoodDocs

Consider it if: specialist AI-assisted HACCP planning is worth a materially higher published site price. Check: currency, local regulatory fit and which features sit in Lite versus Standard.

SafetyCulture

Consider it if: your organisation needs one configurable inspection system across departments or industries. Check: per-seat costs and the work required to create restaurant-specific processes.

Food Safety Guru

Consider it if: access to food-safety specialists and a tailored package matter. Check: total cost and exact included services directly, because no numeric list price was visible.

No universal winner

A small café, a 12-site group and a multinational facilities team do not have the same “best” product. Document must-have workflows and compare like-for-like annual cost.

Method

How this comparison was built.

Inclusion

We included products with a public UK-relevant food-safety, hospitality-operations or inspection offer and enough first-party information to make a useful comparison.

Source hierarchy

Prices, limits and features come from official vendor pages. We did not copy affiliate round-ups or vendor claims about their competitors.

No hidden weighted score

We did not assign points to arbitrary features. The table exposes decision variables so the reader can apply their own priorities.

Commercial disclosure

Blueroll publishes this page and sells one of the products compared. That conflict is why competitors receive direct links and Blueroll's limitations are stated plainly.

What we did not verify

We did not independently audit private security controls, support response times, uptime, implementation quality or every feature behind a login. Absence from the table does not mean a feature is absent.

Maintenance

  • Change one ingredient and inspect how the allergen record updates.
