57% of all recalls are allergen failures.

Not contamination. Not foreign objects. Not expired food. More than half of every food product pulled from UK shelves in 2026 was pulled because the label didn't declare an allergen.

Milk in lamb burgers. Peanuts in chocolate raisins. Wheat in rice. Nuts in coffee syrup. Over and over — the same failure: someone didn't check the allergen matrix against the actual ingredients.

What this means for restaurants.

If major brands with dedicated quality teams are getting allergen labelling wrong 20 times in 3 months, what's happening in kitchens that have no allergen matrix at all?

Under Natasha's Law, every food business must declare all 14 allergens. An EHO inspector will ask your team: "Does this dish contain nuts?" If the answer is "I don't know" — that's a violation.

An accurate, auto-generated allergen matrix — updated every time a recipe changes — is not optional. It's the difference between a recall and a routine inspection.
