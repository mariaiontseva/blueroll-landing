What this means for your kitchen

If any recalled product has passed through your kitchen, you need to document it. A dated note in your HACCP records — "checked stock, product not held / product removed on [date]" — is what your EHO expects to see.

No note = a gap in your records. A gap in your records is what costs you the rating.
