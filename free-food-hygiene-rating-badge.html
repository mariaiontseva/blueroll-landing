Why show your rating?

Diners check hygiene ratings before they book — on the FSA site, on Google, on the sticker by your door. A visible 4 or 5 on your own website answers the question before it's asked, around the clock.

Required by law in Wales and Northern Ireland

Under the Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013 and the equivalent NI legislation, food businesses must display their rating. In England it's voluntary — which is exactly why showing it reads as confidence.

Always up to date

The badge links to your official record, so it reflects every re-inspection with no code changes. Put it up once and forget it exists.

How the 0–5 rating works

Environmental Health Officers score three things: hygienic food handling; the physical condition of the premises; and food safety management — the HACCP procedures, training and temperature records. Most dropped ratings trace back to the third one, the paperwork.

US restaurant grades

In the US, local health departments run the show: NYC letter grades (A/B/C), Chicago Pass/Fail, San Francisco 0–100 scores. The widget speaks all three.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official FSA sticker?

No — the green window sticker is issued by your local authority. This is a web badge that displays your official rating from the FSA's public register, in a design made for websites.

What if my rating changes?

The badge links to your official record and reflects re-inspections — no code changes needed.
