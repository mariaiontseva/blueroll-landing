MULTI-SITE FAQ

Questions restaurant groups ask before rollout

How much does Blueroll cost for multiple sites?

Blueroll costs £24.99 per site per month with unlimited team members. Every site includes the full product, with no enterprise tier, per-seat fee or 12-month contract.

What can head office see compared with a site manager?

Head office can see the complete estate, compare locations and manage shared standards. A site manager can be limited to their own kitchen, team, checklists, incidents and records.

Can every site use the same HACCP standards?

Yes. Restaurant groups can share HACCP standards, recipes, allergen records and checklist templates across the estate while keeping daily checks, temperatures, incidents and deliveries scoped to each location.

Can Blueroll produce records for an EHO inspection?

Yes. Checks are stored with their site, time and sign-off, and can be exported in an inspection-ready report pack for the relevant location.

How long does a multi-site rollout take?

A group can add locations, invite managers and apply shared standards in an afternoon. Blueroll includes onboarding help for teams moving from paper, spreadsheets or another checklist system.

Can a restaurant group move from Trail to Blueroll?

Yes. Blueroll provides free onboarding and helps move sites, checklists and recipes from Trail, paper or spreadsheets. Subscriptions are monthly and can be cancelled at any time.
