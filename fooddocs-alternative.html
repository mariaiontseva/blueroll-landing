Which one fits you

Both replace paper food safety records. The difference is scope and price.

Choose Blueroll if

  • ✓You run one kitchen or a small group and need daily checks, temperature logs, allergens and the records an EHO asks for.
  • ✓You want one price per site, £24.99 a month, with no onboarding fee and no contract.
  • ✓You want the setup done for you from the sheets your team already uses.

Choose FoodDocs if

  • ✓You need traceability, batch-based monitoring and recall data, which FoodDocs lists on Enterprise.
  • ✓You want software to build a full HACCP plan, which FoodDocs offers free, with monitoring from Standard.
  • ✓You produce food in batches and need to trace ingredients through to what you sell.

FoodDocs alternative: questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to FoodDocs for a UK restaurant?

Blueroll is £24.99 a month per site, with unlimited team members and no onboarding fee. When checked from the UK on 25 November 2026, fooddocs.com/pricing listed FoodDocs Standard at £119 a month for the first location billed annually, £89 for the 2nd and 3rd and £69 from the 4th (£149 for one location billed monthly), excluding VAT, with a free HACCP plan builder and Enterprise priced by quote.

Does Blueroll do traceability like FoodDocs?

No. FoodDocs lists traceability logging, batch-based monitoring and recall data on its Enterprise plan. Blueroll covers daily checks, temperature logs, the SFBB diary and HACCP pack, an allergen matrix built from recipes and a one-tap report for the EHO.

Can I try Blueroll before I switch?

Yes. Blueroll is free for 14 days, and signing up on the web at app.blueroll.app needs no card. Send us the sheets you use now and we set up your daily checks for you, free.
