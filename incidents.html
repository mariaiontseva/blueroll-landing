Allergy complaints get special treatment

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to report incidents to the EHO?

Serious incidents (food poisoning allegations, allergen reactions) may need reporting to your local authority — but every incident, big or small, should be in your own records. Inspectors judge the system, not the absence of bad days.

Can staff log incidents without seeing everything else?

Yes — role-based access means kitchen staff can report an incident without access to reports, settings or other sites.

What happens to incident records if a complaint becomes a dispute?

They're timestamped, named and exportable — exactly the evidence you want on your side of the table.
