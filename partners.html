How it works

The kitchen fills it in WhatsApp. You run the quality.

The hardest part of food safety records isn't the template — it's getting a busy team to fill them in honestly, every day. Blueroll moves the filling into the chats the team already uses, and gives you the job you're actually good at: checking it's right.

For the kitchen — no app to install

Checks in the team chat

Opening and closing checks, fridge and freezer temperatures, cooking and hot-hold logs — filled in from WhatsApp or a Telegram group, in a few taps.

Reminders before the deadline

A live list of what's due each morning, and a nudge to whoever hasn't done theirs. Ticked items update for the whole team.

Out of range? Fix it on the spot

A reading outside the limit asks for a corrective action straight away, and the manager is alerted.

Set up once, by you

You choose which checks go to which chat and who's on each team. Staff join from an invite — no accounts, no passwords.

For you — quality control

See every entry, as it's made

Who filled what, when, and from which site. No waiting for the next visit to find a month of blank pages.

Spot entries that don't look real

The same temperature every day, checks ticked minutes before closing, a week filled in at once — flagged for you to look at.

Send it back

Query an entry or ask for a redo — it lands back in the team chat, and you see when it's fixed.

Sign off the week

Your review goes on the record under your name — exactly what an EHO wants to see on inspection day.

Keeping the book

A client who drifts stops paying you

Recurring commission cuts both ways: nobody has a stronger interest in these sites still logging temperatures next year than you do. Blueroll tells you when one starts slipping — straight to your WhatsApp or Telegram, early enough to pick up the phone.

Signals you get — in WhatsApp or Telegram

Daily digest

One message every morning across your whole book: which sites completed their checks, which missed them, and who's falling behind.

Temperature out of range

A fridge logged above 8℃ or a hot hold under 63℃ — you see it the same day, not at your next audit.

Checks stopped

A site that completed its opening checks every day last month, and hasn't this week.

Rating moved

FSA publishes a new score for one of your sites — you hear before the operator does.

New site opened

A group in your book registers another premises. More sites, more commission.

Payment failed

A card bounced. Usually an expired card rather than a decision — but only if someone calls.

The small print, said plainly

What we ask, and what we won't do

This is a single-tier programme

You earn from clients you sign. There is no commission for recruiting other agents, there is no joining fee, and there is nothing to buy. If anyone describes this to you as a network you build underneath yourself, they have it wrong.

Your accounts stay yours

We don't sell over the top of you, and we don't reassign a client you signed.

The rate is personal

Commission follows you, not your company. It isn't transferable or sold on without our agreement.

Servicing keeps the rate

The full share assumes you're the one looking after the client. Hand that back to us and it steps down.

Terms don't change under you

We can change the rate for future signings. Accounts already in your book keep the rate they were signed at.

You carry your own cover

You advise on food safety in your own name. Professional indemnity insurance is on you, as it already is.

Leads are for your territory

Work the list we prepare for you freely. It isn't for resale or for passing to anyone else.
