Keeping the book

A client who drifts stops paying you

Recurring commission cuts both ways: nobody has a stronger interest in these sites still logging temperatures next year than you do. The workspace tells you when one starts slipping, early enough to pick up the phone.

Signals you get

Checks stopped

A site that completed its opening checks every day last month, and hasn't this week.

Rating moved

FSA publishes a new score for one of your sites — you hear before the operator does.

New site opened

A group in your book registers another premises. More sites, more commission.

Payment failed

A card bounced. Usually an expired card rather than a decision — but only if someone calls.

The small print, said plainly

What we ask, and what we won't do

This is a single-tier programme

You earn from clients you sign. There is no commission for recruiting other agents, there is no joining fee, and there is nothing to buy. If anyone describes this to you as a network you build underneath yourself, they have it wrong.

Your accounts stay yours

We don't sell over the top of you, and we don't reassign a client you signed.

The rate is personal

Commission follows you, not your company. It isn't transferable or sold on without our agreement.

Servicing keeps the rate

The full share assumes you're the one looking after the client. Hand that back to us and it steps down.

Terms don't change under you

We can change the rate for future signings. Accounts already in your book keep the rate they were signed at.

You carry your own cover

You advise on food safety in your own name. Professional indemnity insurance is on you, as it already is.

Leads stay in the workspace

Work your territory freely inside the tool. The list itself isn't exportable, and isn't yours to take elsewhere.
