FAQ

Can I create custom roles?

The new roles and permissions system supports role presets and custom roles with capabilities matched to the business.

The group model supports site membership so access can reflect people who work in one or several locations.

Can I see who completed a record?

Completion information is kept with the operational record so managers can review who completed the work and when.
