What the classes mean

FDA · 3 tiers
Class I
Dangerous / Fatal
Reasonable probability of serious health consequence or death. Listeria, Salmonella, undeclared severe allergens.
Class II
Temporary or reversible
Possible temporary health consequence, low probability of serious harm. Foreign objects, minor labeling issues.
Class III
No health threat
Product violates FDA rules but unlikely to cause adverse health effects. Mislabeling, cosmetic issues.
For your records

What this means for your kitchen

If any recalled product passed through your operation, you need to document it. A dated note in your HACCP log — "checked stock, product not held / product removed on [date]" — is what your health inspector expects to see.

No note = gap in your records. Gap in your records = liability if a customer gets sick and traces it back to you.
