The pack

What SFBB is, and what it costs

SFBB is written by the Food Standards Agency (FSA) to help small food businesses run a food safety management system based on HACCP principles, which the law requires. It is published on GOV.UK as PDF packs, and it costs nothing.

Free from the FSA

Download a whole pack or just the sections you need from the SFBB page on GOV.UK. The caterers pack is for restaurants, cafés, takeaways and other small catering businesses.

Packs for different kitchens

There are packs for caterers, retailers, Indian cuisine, Chinese cuisine and childminders, a supplement for residential care homes and teaching resources for colleges. The caterers pack is also in Welsh.

England and Wales

SFBB applies in England and Wales. In Northern Ireland, GOV.UK points businesses to the FSA's Safe Catering guidance. In Scotland, Food Standards Scotland publishes CookSafe.

Each pack has safe methods for cross-contamination, cleaning, chilling and cooking, a management section, and a diary. The safe methods are written once for your kitchen and kept up to date. The diary is the part you fill in every day you open, and it is the part an app can take off paper. Our SFBB guide walks through filling in each section.

The diary

What you fill in every day

The FSA's diary is one page a week, with a 4-weekly review at the back. This is what it asks for.

Every day
  • Opening checks
  • Closing checks
  • Any problems or changes, and what you did about them
  • A name and signature saying your safe methods were followed and supervised that day
Every week

Extra checks, noted in the diary on the day you do them. The FSA's examples are a deep clean, maintenance, cleaning the dishwasher, checking your temperature probe and checking for pests.

Every 4 weeks

A review. Walk round the kitchen, look back over four weeks of diary entries and work through a checklist: safe methods, allergen information, new suppliers, the cleaning schedule, staff training, probe checks and prove it checks. The FSA says to complete it even when nothing went wrong.

Opening checks

  • Fridges, chilled display and freezers are working
  • Other equipment, such as the oven, is working
  • Staff are fit for work and in clean work clothes
  • Food preparation areas are clean and disinfected
  • No sign of pests anywhere
  • Soap, paper towels and sanitiser are stocked
  • Hot running water at every sink and wash basin
  • Probe thermometer works and probe wipes are there
  • Allergen information is right for everything on sale
  • Cleaning was done to the cleaning schedule

Closing checks

  • Food is covered, labelled and in the fridge or freezer
  • Food on its use-by date has been thrown away
  • Dirty cleaning equipment is cleaned or thrown away
  • Waste is out and bins have new bags
  • Food preparation areas are clean and disinfected
  • All washing up is finished
  • Floors are swept and clean
  • Prove it checks have been recorded
  • Cleaning was done to the cleaning schedule

Summarised from the FSA's opening and closing checks safe method, which says you can add your own checks to either list.

The pack also has records you keep up to date: prove it records for probe temperatures, a cleaning schedule, a staff training record, a suppliers' list, a contact list and a safe method completion record.

The FSA says you can print the diary, or download the diary pages to your device, fill them in and store them there. Either way, keep completed pages until your next inspection, because the food safety officer may want to see them as well as your pack.

Questions

SFBB app questions

Is there an official SFBB app?

Not that we could find. When we checked the FSA's SFBB pages on GOV.UK on 18 November 2026, the packs were offered as downloads, and the FSA listed two ways to keep the diary: print it, or download the diary pages to your device, fill them in and store them there. No app was listed. The SFBB apps we looked at, Blueroll included, are made by private companies. Blueroll is not an FSA product and is not endorsed by the FSA.

Is there a free SFBB app?

The SFBB pack itself is free to download from GOV.UK, and the FSA says you can fill in the diary pages on a device. The SFBB apps we checked on 18 November 2026 all charge after a free trial: SFBB+ is £4.99 a month per premises after 90 days free (3 calendar months on iOS), SFBB Pro has a 14-day free trial and then a monthly subscription, and Blueroll is £24.99 a month per site after 14 days free.

Which is the best SFBB app?

It depends on what you need. If you only want the SFBB diary on a phone at the lowest price, SFBB+ costs less than Blueroll. If several people fill in the checks and you also want allergens from your recipes, delivery records, incidents and a one-tap report for the inspector in the same place, Blueroll covers that for £24.99 a month per site. Try any app with the person who fills in your checks before you decide.

Can I use an app instead of the paper SFBB pack?

Yes. The FSA says completed diary pages can be kept electronically or on paper, and the food safety officer may ask to see them along with your SFBB pack at the next visit. What the law asks for is a food safety management system based on HACCP principles, with records of your checks. Blueroll saves each check with the name of the person and the time, and exports your records as a PDF for the inspector. If you are unsure what your business needs, GOV.UK suggests asking your local food safety team.

Do I still need the safe methods if I use an app?

Yes. The diary only records that your safe methods were followed. The methods themselves, how your kitchen avoids cross-contamination, cleans, chills and cooks, still have to fit your kitchen, be agreed and be reviewed. Blueroll's HACCP Pack puts them together from your recipes, cleaning schedules, suppliers and training records, but someone who knows your kitchen still needs to check it and sign it off.

Does Blueroll work in Scotland and Northern Ireland?

SFBB is for England and Wales. In Scotland, Food Standards Scotland publishes CookSafe, and in Northern Ireland, GOV.UK points businesses to the FSA's Safe Catering guidance. Blueroll's checks are not locked to one pack: you can edit the built-in templates or build your own checks with your own safe temperature ranges, so your daily records can follow CookSafe or Safe Catering. For Scotland there is also our free HACCP template for Scottish kitchens.
