Data: Food Standards Agency food hygiene rating scheme (FHRS). Scotland uses the separate Food Hygiene Information Scheme.

What the 0–5 rating means

Environmental Health Officers score three areas at every inspection: hygienic food handling; the physical condition and cleanliness of the premises; and food safety management — the HACCP procedures, training and temperature records that prove the kitchen is under control. A 5 means "very good" compliance in all three; anything below a 4 usually traces back to the paperwork, not the pass.

Ratings are public and re-issued after every inspection. If you're checking your own business and don't like the number, the fastest fixes are usually documentary: an up-to-date HACCP plan, complete fridge logs and allergen records. Our free self-audit shows the gaps in five minutes, and the HACCP template covers the paperwork itself.

How current is the data?

The checker queries the FSA register live — you see exactly what the official record shows, including the latest inspection date.

Why isn't a business showing up?

New businesses appear after their first inspection. Some venues are registered under a different trading name — try the postcode instead.
