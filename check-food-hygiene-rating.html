Why check ratings near you?

Every food business in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland is inspected by local authority Environmental Health Officers and given a hygiene rating from 0 (urgent improvement necessary) to 5 (very good). This map shows those ratings for every restaurant, cafe, takeaway, pub, and food shop near any UK postcode.

For customers

Check before you eat. 89% of consumers recognise the food hygiene rating sticker, but not every business displays it — especially in England, where display is still voluntary. This map lets you see the rating of any business before you visit or order delivery, including the date of the last inspection and the full score breakdown.

For restaurant owners

See how you compare to your neighbours. If competitors around you all have a 5 and you have a 3, customers will notice. Use this tool to identify where you stand and what you need to improve. Our guides on how ratings work and how to improve your rating cover everything you need to know.

About the data

All data comes directly from the Food Standards Agency's FHRS API in real time. Ratings are updated when new inspections are published. The map searches within 1 mile of your postcode and shows up to 100 businesses. For full details on any establishment, visit ratings.food.gov.uk.
