How it works

The team fills it in WhatsApp. You keep an eye on it.

A fire risk assessment is only as good as the records kept after it. In a busy restaurant the weekly alarm test is the first thing to slip. Blueroll moves the fire log book into the chats the team already uses, so your recommendations keep working between visits.

For the client: no app to install

Fire checks in the team chat

Weekly fire alarm tests, monthly emergency lighting and extinguisher checks, fire door and escape route checks and evacuation drills, filled in from WhatsApp or a Telegram group in a few taps.

Reminders before the deadline

A nudge when a check is due and to whoever hasn't done theirs, so the weekly test happens every week.

Something wrong? Recorded on the spot

A failed check, such as a blocked exit or a light that doesn't come on, asks for the action taken straight away, and the manager is alerted.

Food safety in the same place

The same chats carry their daily food safety checks and temperature logs, so the kitchen has one place for all its records.

For you: a view of every client

Morning summary

One message in WhatsApp or Telegram each morning: which of your clients did their fire checks and which didn't. No need to log in to anything.

Client reports

A regular report on each client: what was done, what was missed and what failed. Useful at your next visit and as evidence for the client.

Review reminders

We tell you when a client's fire risk assessment is due for review, so the next visit is booked on time.

The small print, said plainly

What we ask, and what we won't do

This is a single-tier programme

You earn from clients you sign. There is no commission for recruiting other agents, there is no joining fee, and there is nothing to buy.

Your clients stay yours

We don't sell over the top of you, and we don't reassign a client you signed.

Tell us who's yours

Tell us which clients you brought and we record them against your name. That's how we know who to pay.

Your advice stays yours

We carry the records. The fire risk assessment and the advice in it remain yours, and so does your professional indemnity cover.

Terms don't change under you

We can change the amount for future signings. Sites already signed are paid at the amount they were signed at.
