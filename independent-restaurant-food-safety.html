Frequently asked questions

Can it really replace my paper folder?

Yes. Blueroll covers the same daily checks as the FSA's SFBB pack — opening and closing checks, fridge and freezer temperatures, probe calibration, deliveries — as digital checklists with reminders. The difference: a missed check is visible the same day, not discovered at your next inspection.

I'm a one-person kitchen — is this overkill?

Blueroll is lightest exactly there: the built-in templates ARE the setup; you tick the same checks you already do, just on a phone that remembers everything.

Do inspectors accept digital records?

Yes. The law doesn't require paper — inspectors need to see that checks were completed and recorded at the time, by a named person. Blueroll timestamps every check with who did it, and exports your records as a PDF pack you can hand over during an inspection.
