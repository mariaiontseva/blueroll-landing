Why food businesses miss a 5: hygiene scores council by council

Of 303,278 hospitality businesses rated 0 to 5 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, 77.5% hold a 5. Of the 68,175 below a 5, 66.1% scored 10 or more for confidence in management, 61.6% for structure and cleaning and 45.0% for hygienic food handling. A 5 needs every score at 5 or less, so a single score of 10 or more is enough to miss it.

Data: Food Standards Agency, ratings published as of 19 November 2026

By type of business

Takeaways and sandwich shops have the lowest share at 5 (61.8%) and other catering premises the highest (89.3%). Confidence in management is the score most often at 10 or more in every type except pubs, bars and nightclubs, where it is structure and cleaning.

What it means for an owner

Confidence in management is the score most often at 10 or more, ahead of structure and cleaning. It is mostly about your food safety management system and the records that show you follow it: your Safer Food, Better Business pack or HACCP plan, daily checks, temperatures, cleaning and training.

How to get a 5 shows how the three scores set your rating, and the EHO inspection checklist covers what the officer looks at. For the records, start with the free HACCP template and a kitchen cleaning schedule.

Blueroll keeps those records on the team's phones, each check saved with the time and who did it.

Method

Source
Food Standards Agency, Food Hygiene Rating Scheme open data, read through the FHRS API (version 2) on 19 November 2026.
Councils
All 331 local authorities on the 0 to 5 scheme in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, port health authorities included. Scotland is left out because its scheme, FHIS, gives a pass or improvement required rather than a score from 0 to 5.
Business types
Restaurant/Cafe/Canteen, Takeaway/sandwich shop, Pub/bar/nightclub, Mobile caterer, Hotel/bed & breakfast/guest house and Other catering premises, as the FSA classifies them. Other types, such as retailers, manufacturers, schools and care premises, are not counted.
Rated
Businesses with a published rating from 0 to 5, all three inspection scores and a rating date. Exempt businesses, those awaiting inspection or publication, and records missing a score or the date are left out.
Scores
Points against, so 0 is best. Hygienic food handling and structure and cleaning each run from 0 to 25; confidence in management runs from 0 to 30. A 5 needs every score at 5 or less, so a score of 10 or more in an area is what keeps a business below 5.
Percentages
At 5 and below 5 are shares of rated businesses. The three score columns are shares of businesses below 5; one business can count in more than one, so they do not add up to 100%. Figures are rounded to one decimal place, and n/a means no business in that council is below 5.
Days since inspection
The median number of days from the rating date in each FSA record to 19 November 2026.
Limitations
These are the ratings on the register now, and some are years old: nationally the median is 428 days, and 23.5% of ratings are more than 730 days old. A new inspection appears only once the council publishes it. Small councils rest on few businesses: River Tees has 3 rated.
