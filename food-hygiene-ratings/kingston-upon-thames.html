Frequently asked questions

Who inspects food businesses in Kingston upon Thames?

Environmental health officers from Kingston Council. Visits are unannounced, and the new rating is published on the FSA register within a few weeks of the inspection.

My kitchen in Kingston upon Thames got a low rating. How do I get re-inspected?

Fix the issues, then request a re-rating visit through Kingston Council. Officers look hardest at your daily records; our guide to improving your food hygiene rating covers the fastest route back to a 5.

Is a 0 rating illegal?

No. It means urgent improvement is necessary, but the business may keep trading unless the council issues a hygiene emergency prohibition notice.

How current is this page?

The numbers are rebuilt every week from the FSA's open register, and the map and search query it live. A rating changes only when Kingston Council re-inspects.
