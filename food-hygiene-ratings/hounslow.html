Frequently asked questions

Who inspects food businesses in Hounslow?

Environmental health officers from Hounslow Council. Visits are unannounced, and the new rating is published on the FSA register within a few weeks of the inspection.

My kitchen in Hounslow got a low rating. How do I get re-inspected?

Fix the issues, then request a re-rating visit through Hounslow Council. Officers look hardest at your daily records; our guide to improving your food hygiene rating covers the fastest route back to a 5.

Is a 0 rating illegal?

No. It means urgent improvement is necessary, but the business may keep trading unless the council issues a hygiene emergency prohibition notice.

How current is this page?

The numbers are rebuilt every week from the FSA's open register, and the map and search query it live. A rating changes only when Hounslow Council re-inspects.
